# Platform Teams, Shared Capabilities, and Ownership

> A platform team owns a shared capability as a product for internal developers. It
> provides a supported contract, safe defaults, diagnostics, reliability, migration, and
> a feedback loop—not only a common library.

## Quick Recall

- Centralize a capability when consistency and expertise outweigh coordination cost.
- Keep product policy in feature teams unless the platform truly owns it.
- Define provider and consumer responsibilities as a service contract.
- Measure adoption quality, support cost, reliability, and delivery improvement.
- Offer escape hatches without permitting permanent silent forks.
