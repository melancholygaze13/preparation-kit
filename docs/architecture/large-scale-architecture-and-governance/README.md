# Large-Scale Architecture and Governance

Large-scale architecture is the operating system around technical boundaries: who decides,
who owns shared capabilities, and who responds when failures cross teams. This is
role-specific preparation for Staff and Principal interviews.

## Learning Path

1. [Architecture Decisions, Standards, and Exceptions](architecture-decisions-standards-and-exceptions/README.md)
2. [Platform Teams, Shared Capabilities, and Ownership](platform-teams-shared-capabilities-and-ownership/README.md)
3. [Observability, Resilience, and Incident Boundaries](observability-resilience-and-incident-boundaries/README.md)

## Preparation Paths

- **Rapid review:** Read the three overviews and rehearse decision scope, platform product
  ownership, and end-to-end incident accountability.
- **Standard preparation:** Complete all bundles for Staff or Principal system-design and
  leadership interviews.
- **Role-specific depth:** Connect the material to a real cross-team standard, shared
  capability, migration, or incident from your experience.
